How I Got Here

I have been drawn to software since childhood, from early arcade games and a Commodore 64 to teaching myself programming at 16 from a Turbo Pascal book. The path was not perfectly linear, and software engineering was not an obvious route in the environment I grew up in, but the curiosity stayed.

That early interest eventually became a professional path through computer science studies, full-stack engineering, and a long-running Shopify specialization. Today I use CheckoutLabs to offer focused senior development capacity for teams that need someone who can handle the parts that do not fit neatly into a theme setting or off-the-shelf app.
