What Gets Handled

Practical Shopify work that improves the buying experience, clarifies complex product decisions, and keeps operational logic maintainable after launch - delivered white-label when needed.

🏷️

Native B2B Pricing & Rules

Pricing, MOQ, bulk purchasing, and account-specific rules that remove ambiguity from business buying paths and reduce manual follow-up.

🧩

Checkout UI Extensions

Checkout decisions such as delivery dates, shipping constraints, and validation brought into the checkout where they can prevent failed or confusing orders.

👟

Product Configurators

Configurable product experiences that turn complex options into guided choices, helping shoppers understand what they are buying before checkout.

🧠

Conversion Quizzes

Fast, app-light quizzes that guide shoppers toward relevant products and capture useful intent without slowing down the storefront.

🎨

Storefront Redesign Implementation

Figma-to-theme builds and UI refinements that lift perceived quality, make pages easier to scan, and keep the storefront feeling consistent.

🔎

Product Discovery & Filtering

Search, filtering, collection, and recommendation experiences that shorten the path from browsing to a confident product decision.

⚙️

Commerce Operations

Product modelling, capacity rules, ticketing flows, metafields, and admin-supporting structures that keep daily commerce operations reliable.

🔄

Integrations & Data Sync

API integrations, product and inventory sync, metafield workflows, and automation that keep commerce data aligned across connected systems.
