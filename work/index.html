Pennywell Farm

Shopify Ticketing Platform Migration & Booking Flow

Shopify ticketing work for Pennywell Farm focused on making a complex attraction booking journey easier to buy and easier to operate inside Shopify. The work moved a custom ticketing platform onto Shopify, preserved the storefront during launch, then introduced a unified booking flow for day tickets, event tickets, season-ticket-holder reservations, and annual passes. Later work focused on stabilizing the supporting app ecosystem and live ticketing operations.

Commerce focus

Replaced fragmented ticket purchase paths with a clearer Shopify booking journey while keeping capacity, check-in, season-ticket, and operational rules manageable behind the scenes.

  • Migrated a custom ticketing storefront onto Shopify
  • Preserved the existing frontend during initial launch, then implemented the redesigned storefront
  • Built a unified booking flow for day tickets, event tickets, season-ticket-holder reservations, and annual passes
  • Reworked ticket modelling around Shopify product variants, line items, and capacity constraints
  • Stabilized supporting Shopify app infrastructure for capacity, check-in, season tickets, and operational workflows

Edenmoor, formerly Pipers Farm

Checkout UI Extension for Delivery Date Selection

Checkout customization for Edenmoor, formerly Pipers Farm, where delivery date selection needed to happen inside Shopify checkout because postcode, cart contents, and shipping methods determine what dates are valid. The Checkout UI Extension used React, Remix, Node.js, and Shopify UI Extensions, with validation that could block checkout until a valid delivery date was selected.

Commerce focus

Reduced delivery-choice ambiguity by moving the decision into checkout, where postcode, cart contents, shipping methods, and validation can work together before the order is placed.

  • Built a Shopify Checkout UI Extension for delivery date selection
  • Moved delivery date selection into checkout where postcode and shipping context are available
  • Added checkout validation to require a valid delivery date
  • Implemented extension-side shipping method logic based on selected date and delivery constraints
  • Supported additional storefront UI and design implementation work during the engagement
